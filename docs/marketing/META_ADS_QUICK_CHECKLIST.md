## 📊 SUCCESS BENCHMARKS (First 7 Days)

**Good Performance:**
- CTR: > 1.5%
- CPC: < $0.75
- Cost per Sign-up: < $8
- Conversion Rate: > 8%

**Action Required If:**
- CTR < 1%: Change creative
- CPC > $1.50: Tighten targeting
- Zero conversions: Check pixel

---

## 🔧 QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING

**Problem**: Low CTR (< 1%)
**Fix**: Change image, test different headlines

**Problem**: High CTR but no clicks to site
**Fix**: Check website loading speed, verify URL

**Problem**: Clicks but no sign-ups
**Fix**: Improve landing page, simplify sign-up form

**Problem**: "Low audience size" warning
**Fix**: Expand to more countries or broaden interests slightly
